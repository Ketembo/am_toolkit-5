Sequence Logic

The plan follows the matrix pillar order 1→7: identify issues and risks first, then build recovery and back-up capability, stock the critical spares, develop and schedule the right maintenance tactics, formalise FMEA’s, and train the people on everything developed. Week 1 mobilises and baselines; Week 8 re-runs the matrix to measure improvement against that baseline.
